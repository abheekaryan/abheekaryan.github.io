Inkbound PDF & EPUB Reader

Privacy Policy


Introduction

AlterZehn develops Inkbound (Android package com.alterzehn.inkbound), a free app for reading PDF and EPUB documents and marking them up with doodles, highlights, notes and bookmarks. This policy explains what information Inkbound handles and where it goes. In short: Inkbound has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and everything you create stays on your device. By using Inkbound you agree to this policy.


Information We Collect

We (AlterZehn) do not collect any personal information. Inkbound has no sign-in, no servers of its own, and contains no advertising, analytics or crash reporting software. We never receive your documents, annotations or usage data.


Data Stored on Your Device

  • Documents. Copies of the PDF and EPUB files you open are cached in the app's private storage so they open quickly.
  • Annotations. Doodles, highlights, notes and bookmarks are saved in the app's private storage, or written into a PDF file when you choose to save or export it.
  • Library and settings. Your recent documents, the folders you add to the library, and your preferences (such as reading light, colours and text size).
  • Word data cache. If you turn on online word data (see below), the results are cached on your device.

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for Inkbound, so this data is not copied to Google Drive or another device.


File Access

Inkbound only reads files and folders that you choose through Android's system file picker, or that you open in Inkbound from another app. It does not scan your storage. You can revoke a folder’s access at any time in Android settings, or by clearing Inkbound’s data.


Optional Online Word Data

Inkbound includes a built-in, offline word list and dictionary. The optional Use online word data setting (in the ⋮ menu) is off by default, and the app makes no network requests while it is off. When you turn it on, Inkbound uses these free public services to show how common or trending a word is:

  • Datamuse – individual words you annotate or look up that are not in the built-in list, and Word of the Day words, are sent to look up how common they are. (Datamuse API and privacy)
  • Wikimedia (Wikipedia) – Inkbound downloads the public list of the most viewed Wikipedia articles. None of your words are sent. (Wikimedia privacy policy)
  • Google Trends – Inkbound may download the public trending searches feed. None of your words are sent. (Google privacy policy)

Inkbound sends no account, device identifier or other personal identifier with these requests, and never sends your documents. Like any website, these services receive your IP address and standard request information (such as the time of the request and an app identification string, “Inkbound/<version>”). Their handling of that data is governed by their own privacy policies linked above. You can turn the setting off at any time.


Read Aloud (Text-to-Speech)

Read aloud uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your device (for example, Speech Services by Google). Inkbound passes the text to that engine on your device. Inkbound itself does not send it anywhere. Some engines or voices may use the network; that is covered by the engine provider's privacy policy.


Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, share or trade any information, because we do not collect it. Inkbound shows no ads.


Permissions

  • Internet – used only for the optional online word data described above.
  • Read external storage (Android 12 and older only) – to open documents you choose from another app or file manager.

Inkbound does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or accounts.


Data Retention and Deletion

All data stays on your device until you delete it. You can remove items in the app, or delete everything by clearing Inkbound's storage in Android settings (Settings → Apps → Inkbound → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the app. Files you saved or exported to your own folders are not removed and remain under your control. We hold no data on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete.


Data Security

App data is kept in Inkbound's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Online word data requests use encrypted connections (HTTPS).


Children's Privacy

Inkbound does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, and is suitable for all ages.


Cookies

The app does not use cookies.
