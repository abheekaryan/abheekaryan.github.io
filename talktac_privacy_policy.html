Privacy Policy


Introduction

AlterZehn develops TalkTac (Android package com.alterzehn.talktac), a free app that combines two-player online games with group chat and user stories. This policy explains what information TalkTac collects, how it is used, who processes it, and how you can delete it. By using TalkTac you agree to this policy.


Information We Collect

  • Account information. You can sign in with Google or continue as a guest. Both use Firebase Authentication (Google). With Google sign-in we receive your Google account's email address, name and profile picture. Guests get an anonymous account with no personal details. Each account has a unique user ID (UID).
  • Profile. The username you choose and your profile picture.
  • Images and stories. Profile pictures and story images you upload are stored on Cloudinary, our image host. Stories expire automatically after about 12 hours and their images are then removed.
  • Game data. Scores, match results, recent players, online presence and game invitations, stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database.
  • Chat messages. Group chat and in-game messages are relayed in real time by our game server hosted on Render. Messages are not stored on the server after delivery. If you report a message, its text is saved with the report as evidence.
  • Device identifier. A pseudonymous device ID derived from your device's Android ID is stored with your account to enforce bans and prevent abuse.
  • Push notification tokens. Firebase Cloud Messaging tokens, used to send you game invitations and notifications.
  • App logs and crash reports. When errors or crashes occur, a short, masked error description (with your user ID, app version and device model) is uploaded to our database so we can fix problems.
  • IP addresses. Our servers and service providers (Google Firebase, Render, Cloudinary) receive your IP address as part of normal network communication.
  • Reports, blocks and moderation data. Reports you make or receive (including the reported message text or image), user blocks you set, ban records and report limits.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the games, chat, stories, profiles and notifications.
  • To keep the community safe: profanity filtering in chat, reports, blocking, bans and device bans.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve the app.

On-Device Image Check

Before you upload a profile picture or story, TalkTac runs an automatic nudity check on your device. This check happens entirely on your phone; the image is not sent anywhere for it.


Blocking and Reporting

You can block other users so their messages, invitations and presence are hidden from you, and you can report users, messages and stories. Reports are reviewed by our moderators, who may ban accounts or devices that violate our rules.


Sharing Your Information

TalkTac shows no ads and we do not sell or trade your personal information. Your data is processed only by our service providers to run the app: Google Firebase (authentication, database, notifications), Cloudinary (image hosting) and Render (game and chat server). Your username, profile picture, scores, stories and chat messages are visible to other users. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the safety of users.


Data Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to the database is restricted by security rules and server-side token verification.


Data Retention

  • Account, profile and game data are kept until you delete your account.
  • Stories are removed after about 12 hours.
  • Chat messages are not stored after delivery, except as report evidence.
  • Guest accounts' data is deleted when the guest logs out.
  • Reports, bans and device ban records may be kept for up to 12 months to keep the community safe, and are then deleted.

Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account in the app from Profile or About → Delete account. This removes your profile, images, scores and other data linked to your account. You can also request deletion by emailing alterzehn02@gmail.com. See how to delete your account.


Children's Privacy

TalkTac is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Guest users cannot use chat. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.


Permissions

TalkTac uses Internet and network state access, and the notification permission to show invitations. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files other than images you choose to upload.


Cookies

The app does not use cookies.
