Delete your TalkTac account

TalkTac (Android, by Alterzehn) lets you delete your account and its data at any time.

Delete in the app

  1. Open TalkTac and sign in.
  2. Open your Profile and tap Delete account (also under About > Delete account).
  3. Confirm. For security the app checks it's you again (Google sign-in, or your password if you use email sign-in), then removes your data and deletes your account.

Request by email

If you can't use the app, email alterzehn@gmail.com with the subject TalkTac account deletion request from the email address you used to sign in (your Google account or your email sign-in address). We complete requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (Google or email sign-in and user ID)
  • Display name, profile photo link, score and game stats
  • Your stories, online/recent player entries, blocked-users list, bookmarks and notification tokens
  • Your music status and daily music and voice usage counts, chat cooldown and Premium record
  • The profile pictures, story images and music clips you uploaded with current versions of the app (stored on Cloudinary)

What is kept

To keep the community safe, records of reports (including reported message text), bans and device bans, and the device records and report limits used to enforce them, may be kept for up to 12 months and are then deleted. Masked error reports linked to your user ID are not removed automatically; email us and we will remove them. Images uploaded with very old versions of the app (before Cloudinary) cannot be traced automatically; email us to have them removed. Copies that other players' phones have already cached may remain on those phones until their app clears its cache. Group chat messages are not stored after delivery and voice is never recorded. Guest sessions and their data are deleted automatically when you log out.
