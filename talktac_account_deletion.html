Delete your TalkTac account

TalkTac (Android, by Alterzehn) lets you delete your account and its data at any time.

Delete in the app

  1. Open TalkTac and sign in.
  2. Open your Profile and tap Delete account (also under About > Delete account).
  3. Confirm. The app signs you in again for security, removes your data and deletes your account.

Request by email

If you can't use the app, email alterzehn02@gmail.com with the subject TalkTac account deletion request from the Google account you used to sign in. We complete requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (Google sign-in link and user ID)
  • Display name, profile photo link, score and game stats
  • Your stories, online/recent player entries, blocked-users list and notification token

What is kept

To keep the community safe, records of reports, bans and device bans, and the device records and report limits used to enforce them, may be kept for up to 12 months and are then deleted. Group chat messages are not stored after delivery. Guest sessions are deleted automatically when you log out.
